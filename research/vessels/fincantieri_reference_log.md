# Fincantieri-group reference log (vessels 2000+)

Purpose: scope and plausibility reference for the Software Defined Vessel "resource governor". Real-vessel data here is for plausibility checks only. Our code uses only the challenge's own numbers. Everything below comes from public sources: Wikipedia/Wikimedia Commons, GAO/CRS, navy and coast guard sites, Fincantieri/Vard pages, and trade press. Combat systems are only named, never described. Compiled 2026-10-16.

Conventions: **WP** = English Wikipedia. **(excerpt)** = seen only in a search-result excerpt; the page itself was not opened. **unconfirmed** = could not be verified. Numbers that disagree across sources are shown side by side. Bracketed numbers [n] point to Section 6.

**Plausibility flag.** Our fictional 60–70 m vessel carries two mission bays, a flight deck with hangar, a stern ramp and three masts. A hangar for one VTOL *drone*, rather than a crewed helicopter, keeps the brief credible. The ramp-plus-bay layout is real on the 77.5 m VARD 7 078 design.

**Mission and payload provisions.** These come from WP [1], consistent with Naval News and navypedia excerpts [3].
- Aft mission bay under the flight deck: up to 5 ISO 1C (20-ft) containers or 3 RHIBs over 11 m.
- Midship bay: up to 8 ISO 1C containers or 2 RHIBs.
- One 20 t crane for the containers.
- Stern ramp for RHIBs over 11 m, plus 2 × 10 t side davits.
- Flight deck 25.5 m × 16.5 m. Double hangar for 2 × SH90 or 1 × EH101.
- Masts and antennas: an integrated sensor mast (configuration-dependent radar fit), dual-band and tri-band SATCOM antennas, and a SAT-TV antenna [1]. A combat system is fitted; not described here.

**Automation and manning.** A Leonardo "naval cockpit" integrated bridge runs in a cockpit layout operable by two crew [1][2]. Light, Light+ and Full share one hull; the lighter fits are "fitted for but not with" the extra systems and can be upgraded later [1]. That is the real-world pattern for pre-provisioned space, weight, power and cooling slots.

**Public diagrams.** No freely licensed general arrangement, profile or cutaway was found. Baird Maritime's review carries Italian Navy and Fincantieri photos (link-only) [2].

### 2.2 Freedom-class LCS (Fincantieri Marinette Marine), with the Saudi MMSC derivative, relevance 5

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Builder / yard | Marinette Marine (Fincantieri Marinette Marine), Wisconsin; Lockheed Martin is prime contractor [5][9] |
| Years | Construction 2005–; commissioned 2008–; 16 built, 6 retired [5] |
| Length / beam / draft | 115 m / 17.5 m / 3.9 m [5] |
| Displacement | ~3,500 t full load [5]. GAO, Jan 2014: LCS-1 naval-architectural limit 3,400.0 t against a full-load condition of 3,374.2 t [7] |
| Crew | Core crew 40–50, about 75 with the mission and aviation detachments [5]. Later practice: about 70 core plus a 23-person air detachment. The ships have headroom for 3-high bunks (up to about 100), and berthing modules have been installed *inside mission bays* [6] |
| Propulsion | 2 × Rolls-Royce MT30 (36 MW total); 2 × Colt-Pielstick 16PA6B diesels (6.8 MW as stated); 4 waterjets [5] |
| Generators | 4 × Isotta Fraschini diesel gensets: 750 kW each (3 MW) in WP body text, 800 kW each in the infobox [5] |
| Electrical | WP calls electrical-system problems among the class's most serious [5] |
| Cooling | No public mission-package cooling allocation found |

**Mission and payload provisions** [5][6][7][8]
- Reconfigurable mission bay with a three-axis crane for moving modules and cargo.
- Boats and unmanned surface vessels launch and recover over a stern ramp or through a starboard side door near the waterline.
- Flight deck with integrated helicopter handling and a Trigon traversing system. Hangar for 1 MH-60 plus 2 MQ-8B, or 1 MQ-8C.
- Mission systems ride in ISO 10-ft or 20-ft support containers or on flat racks. The Mission Package Computing Environment is the interface to the ship (excerpt) [8].
- **Weight budget:** a mission package must stay within 180 t installed. Of that, 105 t is for equipment and 75 t is fuel to power it. LCS requirements set no service-life allowance for the packages themselves [7].
- **Contention example (GAO).** Space and weight limits meant the increment-4 mine-countermeasures package could carry the Knifefish UUV *or* the influence-sweep USV system, not both at once. The ASW package was about 4 t over its 105 t cap [7]. This is the admission-control problem our governor solves.
- **Reconfiguration time.** The Independence variant swapped from mine countermeasures to surface warfare in 96 h (2014). In 2016 the Navy dropped routine swapping and fixed each ship division to one mission [6].

**Saudi MMSC.** Freedom hull form, 118.6 m, about 3,600 t. It drops the modular-package capability and the two forward mission bays, keeping a fixed aft mission bay with stern ramp. First ship *Saud* (820) launched 16 Dec 2025 [9].
